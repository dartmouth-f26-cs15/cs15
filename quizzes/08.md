What are the values in the registers after executing the following instructions?

For each `push` instruction, indicate the value that it writes into memory, and at what address.

```asm
mov rax, 5
mov rcx, 6
mov rsp, 0xda1e10
push rax
push rcx
shr rax, 3
pop rdi
```
